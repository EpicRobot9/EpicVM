WHY THE RunSynchronous ACCOUNT WAS NOT CREATED (proven from the image)
  Windows\Panther\actionqueue\specialize.uaq was generated and processed, but its
  contents are ONLY two actions:
     SETUPUGC.EXE  (identity Microsoft-Windows-Deployment)
     RUNDLL32.EXE shsetup.dll,SHUnattendedSetup
  A direct string search of that queue for 'net user' returns: DOES NOT CONTAIN.
  The log shows SETUPUGC.EXE was 'Executing', but no RunSynchronousCommand
  execution line exists anywhere in setupact.log, and the oobeSystem pass never
  appears in the log at all.

     commands were never expanded or executed. The account was never created.
  The 'session might have ended' error is consistent with PowerShell Direct
  reaching a guest that is still in Setup (Heartbeat 'No Contact'), not a
  working OS with a bad password.

SUPPORTING FACT: a DISM-applied image has no SetupComplete marker, so Windows
Setup re-runs on EVERY boot until something marks installation complete. The
leftover unattend.xml on the OS volume is re-read on each boot, which is why the
'Install Windows' dialog reappeared. Removing the unattend after first boot is
necessary but was NOT sufficient, because the account is still missing.

CONCLUSION
  The unattend-only approach has now failed twice for two different reasons
  (file deleted too early; RunSynchronous never expanded). The gap is that a
  DISM-applied image still needs a normal first-boot OOBE completion before it is
  a usable, credential-reachable OS. A DISM+unattend build cannot skip that by
  itself.
